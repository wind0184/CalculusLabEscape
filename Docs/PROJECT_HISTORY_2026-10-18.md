# Calculus Lab Escape — current gameplay build

Current direction: **develop Differentiation and Integration UI/gameplay first; defer login and later integrate Firebase**. The previous ASP.NET/SQLite login experiment is retained for reference and regression, not used by normal play.

## Run

- Double-click `../Start-LearningLab.cmd`, or run `Builds/LearningLab/CalculusLabEscape.exe`.
- Unity 6000.3.10f1: open `Assets/_Project/Scenes/MainMenu.unity` and press Play. No backend or account setup is required.
- Choose a mission. All three missions are available. **Continue** resumes the most recent unfinished local session, including Differentiation and Integration.
- WASD / arrows move, mouse looks, E inspects the active device, Esc pauses or closes the current support/manual overlay.

- **Integration:** finish Exponent Dial first, including its two independent questions. Then use Wires, Sequence and Pressure in any order; each also includes two independent questions. - Incorrect attempts count towards accuracy without taking hearts or clearing accepted work. Basic hints and the manual are free; stronger hints cost one of three hearts.
- Wrong-answer feedback identifies the control or concept to revisit: coefficients, exponents, root/reciprocal rewrites, operation order, selected antiderivatives or +C. Results group accepted steps, first-try answers and incorrect attempts by skill, and suggest practice from the most frequent recorded error. Learning records retain the feedback shown on each new submission; older records remain readable.
- Results now offer **Practise this focus / 3 questions**: one of seven authored sets, selected from the recorded error category (general topic practice if no specific set applies). Each question scores its first submission and then explains the answer. Practice evidence is saved separately within the session and does not alter mission accuracy, time, hearts or completion. **Replay mission** still starts the full level again.
- **Learning records → completed Differentiation / Integration session** now offers **Start focused practice**, **Continue practice** or **View practice review**, depending on its latest set. Continue restores submitted answers after returning to the menu or restarting the game; the original mission is not reloaded. Closing the panel refreshes the same record.
- Mission and practice expressions now display powers as superscripts and `sqrt(x)` as a square root. This includes prompts, previews, wire choices, explanations and saved-record display. Number/fraction input and stored answers remain plain text; no grading rules or historical values are rewritten.

Local records remain in `Application.persistentDataPath/Chapter4Sessions`, under the `Local player` profile. They are not Firebase records and do not merge authenticated test records. Saved state includes accepted steps, support, active time, case countdown and bridge environmental state; drafts and exact player position are not restored.

Both mission sidebars show **NEXT ACTION**, following the current chip, answer, installation, wire, sequence slot or shutdown state. **How to play / Free** explains the current controls without submitting an answer or charging hearts. Switching focus preserves the draft and does not submit it; accepted repairs still require installation. Selected chips, wire sources and procedure cards show **[X]** with a cyan border. Clicking and dragging share the same selection state; accepted items stop showing a pending selection. See [PRODUCTION_PHASE14.md](History/PRODUCTION_PHASE14.md).
